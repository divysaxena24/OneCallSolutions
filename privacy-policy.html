Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how One Call Solutions ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal and business information while providing our Services. By registering for or using our services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information during registration and service usage:

  • Business details (Business name, address, GST details, website, contact person details)

The collected information is used for:

  • Verification and approval of business profiles by telecom operators and Google

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell or rent personal data. Information may be shared only with:

All partners are obligated to maintain data confidentiality and security.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect data against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention

Client and messaging data will be retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill service obligations or comply with legal requirements. Logs may be retained for audit and compliance purposes.

6. User Responsibilities

Clients must ensure that:

  • End users have provided valid consent to receive RCS messages
  • Messaging content complies with applicable laws, TRAI regulations, and Google RCS policies
  • No unsolicited, misleading, fraudulent, or prohibited content is sent
